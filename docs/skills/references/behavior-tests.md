# Behavioral regression tests

These scenarios verify generic adviser behaviour. They are not user-specific business rules.

## T1 - Increase SIP while major future EMI is approaching
Expected: calculate/estimate future mandatory outgo first; approve only if emergency reserve and near-term commitments remain protected. May approve with guardrails rather than blindly increasing SIP.

## T2 - Buy a second property while first large mortgage is still ramping up
Expected: model future debt service, down payment, furnishing/registration, liquidity and income-loss stress. Usually defer/reject if current profile cannot absorb the second obligation.

## T3 - Prepay mortgage or invest
Expected: compare guaranteed interest saved versus expected after-tax investment return, liquidity, horizon, goal shortfalls and risk. Allow mixed strategy. Never decide from headline return alone.

## T4 - Switch an established mutual fund because its recent ranking fell
Expected: do not switch on ranking alone. Route scheme analysis to Mutual Fund Mentor if possible; examine process, rolling risk-adjusted results, overlap, tax and exit load; final decision considers household goals.

## T5 - Attractive stock or IPO conflicts with near-term goal
Expected: HM Bulls may rate the security attractive, while holistic adviser can still reject/defer purchase because earmarked near-term capital is unavailable for equity risk.

## T6 - Nine-month job loss
Expected: recalculate emergency runway using mandatory outgo including EMI; suspend discretionary investments before protection/essentials; identify when reserves run out and what structural changes become necessary.

## T7 - Synthetic age 55, five years to retirement, high behavioural tolerance but low corpus and large obligations
Expected: risk capacity overrides tolerance. Do not increase equity merely to catch up. Show contribution/date/spending trade-offs.

## T8 - Synthetic age 30, high income, aggressive behaviour, no emergency fund and uninsured dependants
Expected: do not recommend aggressive investing first. Liquidity and protection precede portfolio optimization despite long horizon.

## T9 - Paid-in-full credit card usage
Expected: treat as spending mechanism, not debt, unless statement balances revolve or interest-bearing EMI exists.

## T10 - Earmarked emergency FD also listed as an asset
Expected: include in net worth but exclude from freely investable capital unless explicitly reallocated.
